**Caveats**
- Coverage: 24 evaluated, 3 parsed below 0.6 and are in Review.
- Method: full read of each resume against a frozen rubric, blind to identity, one resume per scoring context.
- Unverified gates: g1 (office days) for 21 candidates.
- Guardrails NOT RUN: none.
- Reviewers: evidence-integrity passed; fairness-audit passed, including a name-swap spot check on 3 candidates.
- This is a recommendation to support a human reviewer. It is not a hiring decision.
