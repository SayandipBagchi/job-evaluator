# Candidate notice template

A starting draft for the deploying organisation's legal team. Fill the bracketed parts, and have counsel approve the final text before use. Do not change approved text afterwards without counsel.

**How we review applications for [role title]**

We use a software tool to help our recruiters review applications for this role. The tool compares the experience described in your resume against the requirements in the job description and suggests which applications a recruiter should read first.

- **What it looks at:** the work experience, skills and outcomes in your resume, compared with the published requirements for the role.
- **What it does not look at:** your name, photo, age, gender, contact details or any other personal characteristic. These are removed before the comparison.
- **Who decides:** a recruiter reviews every suggestion and makes every decision. The tool never rejects an application.
- **Data and retention:** we use your resume only to assess your application for this role [and similar roles, if you agreed]. We keep a record of the assessment, not a copy of your resume in the tool, for [retention period].
- **Your choices:** you can ask for your application to be reviewed without the tool, ask how it was assessed, or ask us to delete your data, by writing to [contact].

[NYC roles only: An independent bias audit of this tool was completed on [date]. A summary is available at [link].]
