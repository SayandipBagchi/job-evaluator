# Worked rubric

**Input JD (abridged):** Senior Product Manager, Card Issuing. Hybrid, Bengaluru, 3 days in office. "Own the roadmap for our card issuing platform. Partner with risk, compliance and engineering to launch new card programmes. 6+ years in product, ideally in fintech. Strong SQL and data skills. Experience with regulated launches (RBI or equivalent). MBA from a top institute preferred. We are a young, energetic team."

## What comes back

The biggest weighting call: this is a launch-and-stakeholder role, so shipping regulated card programmes carries more than SQL depth, even though the JD lists SQL early.

Signals total 100. Weak signals total 3, under the cap of 5.

**Translated, not scored:**
- "6+ years in product" is not a separate criterion. Years stand in for the capability that s1 to s4 already measure, so they are scored there.
- "Top institute" is dropped. The MBA itself stays as a weak signal; the institute's rank does not.
- "young, energetic team" is dropped. It is an age proxy, not a capability.

**Open question for the hiring manager:** is the 3-day office requirement firm? If not, g1 should not be a gate.
